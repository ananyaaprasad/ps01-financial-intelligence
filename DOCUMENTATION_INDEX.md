## ❓ FAQ

### Q: How do I enable dark mode?
**A**: Click the moon/sun icon in the top-right header corner.

### Q: Will my changes be saved?
**A**: Theme preference and tab selection persist during your session.

### Q: Is the data real?
**A**: No, all data is clearly marked as SIMULATED for demo purposes.

### Q: Can I edit/modify the dashboard?
**A**: Yes! The code is in `dashboard.html` - edit directly or review [TECHNICAL_GUIDE.md](TECHNICAL_GUIDE.md).

### Q: What if something doesn't work?
**A**: Try refreshing the page. Check browser console (F12) for errors.

### Q: Can I deploy this to production?
**A**: Yes, but connect it to real APIs first. See [TECHNICAL_GUIDE.md](TECHNICAL_GUIDE.md).
